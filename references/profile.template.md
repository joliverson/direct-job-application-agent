## Employment and education

For each role, record employer, official title, dates, location, responsibilities, measurable outcomes, and source document or conversation. Record degrees and required credentials precisely.

## Project evidence

For each project, record the problem, your contribution, tools used, production status, measurable result, and whether the details may be shared publicly. Mark unverified claims as unverified until confirmed.

## Limits

Record important gaps or claims that must not be made, such as an unearned degree, certification, clearance, formal management authority, or unsupported technology experience.
